Guide & support

A record for
every next time.

Keep the actual work and the next date together. No account is required.

Version 1.0(2) was submitted October 25, 2026 and is Waiting for Review. It has not been approved or released. Screens show original fictional examples.

Start with one home

In Homes, choose Add home. Enter a name, optional private address/notes, time zone, hemisphere and reporting currency. Free supports one active home and eight active tasks. Archived records remain accessible.

Choose your own maintenance dates

Open a home and choose Add a task. Start with an editable template or a custom title. Set a first date and, if useful, a recurrence in days, weeks, months or years. Choose whether future dates follow the actual completion or your original schedule. Intervals are yours; follow the relevant manufacturer instructions and qualified professional guidance.

Record actual completed work

Open a task and choose Record completed service. Enter the actual date and optional cost/currency, provider, part/model, notes and receipt reference. A missing cost and a recorded zero are different. Review the calculated next date, then save. The service and schedule update as one local transaction. Skipped dates do not become invented service events.

Find and correct a record

History searches service, home, provider, part and reference text. Open a service to retrieve its saved facts. Use Correct service for a correction and review the effect on the next due date. Undo restores the previous successful in-session change. Recoverable Trash retains records and sources; no permanent-delete control is provided.

Keep photos and original PDFs

With Pro, open a home’s Documents & photos, choose a role and Add owned source. Choose Camera, an owned photo or Files, review the prepared source and choose Use source. Cancel leaves your record unchanged. Photos are normalized locally; original PDFs retain supplied metadata. Before/after are your chosen labels. Open Documents & photos to preview, save, rename, replace or move a source to recoverable Trash. Previous originals stay recoverable, and retained sources remain readable and exportable if Pro ends.

Photos are resized to at most 1600 pixels and 2 MB. Original PDFs may be up to 20 MB and 100 unencrypted pages. Device protection limits include 1000 homes, 100000 combined records, 5000 retained sources, 11 GiB retained source data and 12 GiB per full backup.

Preview and save a report

Open a home’s Export screen. Choose A4 or Letter and explicitly choose private fields to include. Free produces the complete basic PDF for that home. Pro adds multi-home/date/category selections, source-reference lists, selected photo pages and CSV. Preview the PDF before sharing or saving through its native controls. Different currencies remain separate; no tax advice or conversion is provided.

Back up and recover

In Settings, choose Save full attachment backup, then select a Files destination you control. Backups include all retained records, private fields, original sources and Trash. Keep a copy outside the app’s storage before deleting the app. To restore a saved backup, choose Import backup as archived copies. Current work remains; each import receives fresh identifiers and starts archived, with imported reminders off.

Generic reminders

Enable local notifications in Settings and approve the system prompt. Enable reminders for the relevant tasks. Only the nearest 50 eligible future task dates are scheduled, using generic text. Refresh status after changing permission. iOS, Focus, notification settings and the device control delivery; a scheduled count is not a delivery receipt.

Annual, Lifetime and Restore Purchases

Settings opens the upgrade screen with current Apple prices. Annual Pro is a one-year auto-renewing subscription; Lifetime Pro is a one-time non-consumable for this app. Buying Lifetime does not automatically cancel Annual. Manage or cancel with Apple. After changing devices or reinstalling, use Restore Purchases with the Apple account that purchased Pro. Pending, cancelled or failed purchases do not grant access. Existing records remain if Pro ends.

Apple Standard Terms · Privacy policy

Contact support

Email hello@arvinify.com with the app version, device/iOS version and the steps that led to the problem. Describe any visible error. Do not send passwords or Apple credentials. Files and home records are never attached automatically; share only the minimum appropriate information.
