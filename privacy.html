Privacy policy

Home records.
Your choices.

Tendarchive processes your maintenance records in protected local app storage.

Effective October 25, 2026 · Tendarchive 1.0 · Publisher: Sepideh Javanbakht Dalir

Version 1.0(2) was submitted October 25, 2026 and is Waiting for Review. It has not been approved or released. App Store privacy responses are published as Data Not Collected.

Local records and sources

Home names and addresses, time zones, tasks, schedules, completed services, costs, contractor/contact details, part references, notes, seasonal choices and retained photos/PDFs are processed locally. Tendarchive has no developer account, backend, advertising, tracking or analytics integration that receives those records.

Camera permission is requested when you choose Camera. Apple’s photo picker provides only the images you select; the app does not request broad Photos read access. Selected photos are normalized locally, resized and stripped of private camera/location metadata. Technical dimensions and color information may remain. Original PDFs retain their supplied bytes and metadata. User-selected Save Image may request add-only Photos permission.

Optional local notifications

Notifications require your opt-in and system permission. Generic wording omits home names, addresses, task details and provider information. Only the nearest 50 eligible future task dates are scheduled in their saved time zones. iOS and device settings control delivery. Tendarchive does not send remote push notifications or generate a professionally verified maintenance schedule.

Apple purchases

Apple manages Annual Pro, Lifetime Pro and Restore Purchases. The app reads verified StoreKit entitlements on the device. Apple credentials and payment details are not entered into or sent to the developer through Tendarchive. Apple’s account, purchase and App Store practices apply separately.

Reports, Files and backups

You choose whether and where to save or share. Private addresses, home/service notes, provider phone/email and receipt references start excluded from PDF reports. Only deliberately selected photos appear in a Pro photo appendix. Preview reports and inspect exported files before sharing. Full attachment backups include private records, every retained source and Trash. Protect any copies you save.

Your chosen Files providers, receiving apps and operating-system/device backups use their own settings and policies. Tendarchive does not implement developer cloud sync or automatically upload records or diagnostics.

Retention and recovery

Recoverable Trash retains records and linked source files. Source replacements retain earlier originals. Trash continues to count toward retained storage limits. Backup imports create fresh archived copies, preserve current work and leave imported task reminders off. The app has no permanent-delete control.

Deleting the app removes its local app storage. Save a full backup outside that storage first if you need your records. Exported copies, support emails and device backups remain managed separately by you and the relevant providers. If saved metadata cannot be read, the app preserves the original before explicit recovery.

Support

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to address your request. The app does not automatically attach home records, photos, backups or diagnostics. Send only what is necessary; do not send passwords, Apple credentials or private home documents unless appropriate.

This website

This static website contains no advertising or analytics script. GitHub Pages hosts it; the hosting provider’s privacy statement applies to website requests separately from local maintenance records.

Questions and changes

Contact hello@arvinify.com with questions. We will update this policy and applicable App Store responses when practices change.
